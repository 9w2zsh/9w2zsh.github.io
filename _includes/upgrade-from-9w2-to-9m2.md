When your callsign changes from 9W2 to 9M2, your existing DMR ID number remains the same, so you should submit an Official Call Change request on RadioID.net rather than creating a new account

1. Update Your Callsign on RadioID.net

1. Log in to your existing account. Sign in to RadioID.net using your current 9W2 credentials.
2. Open your identity record. Navigate to My RadioID → Identities and select your existing DMR ID.
3. Launch the callsign change form. Click or tap the Call field displaying your 9W2 callsign, then select Open Callsign Change Form in the Official Call Change window.
4. Submit your MCMC proof. Enter your new 9M2 callsign and license expiration date, attach a copy of your updated MCMC Apparatus Assignment (AA) showing the 9M2 callsign, and submit. (Avoid using "Update Identity" or registering a second account, as your 7-digit DMR ID stays tied to you.)

2. Sync Related Networks & Hardware

Once RadioID approves your change (usually within 24–48 hours), update the rest of your digital setup:
• BrandMeister Network. Wait a few minutes after RadioID updates so the database syncs, then register a new BrandMeister SelfCare account using your new 9M2 callsign. According to the BrandMeister Callsign Change Guide, your old 9W2 account will be removed within 2–3 weeks while keeping your original DMR ID.
• Hotspots (Pi-Star / WPSD). Open your hotspot's configuration dashboard, change the callsign field from 9W2 to 9M2, and run a database/hostfile update so your new callsign displays properly on dashboards.
• DMR Radio Codeplug. Open your radio's CPS (Customer Programming Software), update your Radio Name / Callsign from 9W2 to 9M2 (leaving your Radio ID unchanged), and write the updated codeplug to your transceiver.
