Here is your quick-reference cheat sheet for operating and programming the Icom IC-2720H dual-band mobile transceiver. [1]

🎛️ Essential Front Panel Controls
Power On/Off: Press and hold the [PWR] button for 1 second.
Select Main Band: Press the [MAIN●BAND] knob/switch on either the Left (VHF) or Right (UHF) side to designate it as your primary control band.
Lock/Unlock Control: Press and hold [SET●LOCK] for 1 second to toggle keypad lock on or off.
Switch Power Output: Press [LOW●PRIO] to cycle through High, Mid, and Low power settings.
Monitor (Squelch Open): Press and hold [DUP●MONI] for 1 second to manually open the squelch and listen for weak signals. [1, 2, 3]

📻 Basic Programming (VFO Mode)
Select the Band: Press the Left or Right [MAIN●BAND] knob to choose your desired side. [1, 2]
Enter VFO Mode: If you are in Memory mode, press the [V/MHz] button on the front panel (or [D] on the HM-133 mic) to switch to VFO.
Set the Frequency: Rotate the [DIAL] knob to tune, or press [C] on the HM-133 microphone and type in the frequency directly (e.g., 1 4 6 7 8 0). [1, 2]
Set Repeater Offset (Duplex): Press [DUP●MONI] to cycle through DUP– (minus shift), DUP+ (positive shift), and Simplex (no offset). [1, 2]
Set PL/CTCSS Subaudible Tone:
Press [TONE●DTMF] repeatedly until "T" appears on the screen (enables transmit tone).
To change the tone frequency: Press [SET●LOCK] to enter the Set Menu, rotate the dial until rT (repeater tone) displays, select your subaudible tone frequency (e.g., 100.0 Hz), and press [SET●LOCK] again to exit. [1, 2]

💾 Saving a Frequency to Memory
While in VFO mode, tune in your desired frequency, duplex offset, and tone settings.
Press [M/CALL●MW] briefly. The memory channel number on the display will start flashing.
Rotate the [DIAL] knob to select the target channel number you want to save into.
Press and hold [M/CALL●MW] for 1.5 seconds until you hear three beeps. The settings are now stored. [1, 2]

🎤 HM-133 Microphone Quick Keys Using the HM-133 DTMF microphone gives you immediate access to functions without touching the radio base: [1, 2]
[A] (FUNC): Accesses secondary button choices.
[B] (VFO): Toggles VFO mode.
[C] (MR): Toggles Memory Recall mode.
[D] (CALL): Selects the programmed Call Channel.
[SQL▲ / SQL▼] (1 & 2): Adjusts squelch levels.
[VOL▲ / VOL▼] (Ref/4): Adjusts volume levels.
[BAND] (Enter): Switches the Main Band control between left and right sides. [1, 2]

🔄 Cross-Band Repeater Activation
To use your IC-2720H as a local cross-band repeater node: [1]
Set your VHF frequency on one band and your UHF frequency on the other band.
Set your desired squelch levels so both sides are completely quiet.
Press and hold [SET●LOCK] while turning the radio power ON.
Look for the small repeater icon/indicator flashing on the screen to confirm loop activation.
To exit: Turn the radio off, then hold down [SET●LOCK] again while turning it back on. [1]
