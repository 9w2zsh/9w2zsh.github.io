🎬 STEP 2: PROMPT #1
PRODUCT CINEMATIC REVEAL
Mula-mula, kita nak jadikan gambar produk biasa nampak macam iklan premium.
Upload gambar produk yang jelas menggunakan pilihan "Ingredients".
Kemudian paste prompt ini:
"Create a premium 8-second cinematic product commercial using the uploaded reference image as the primary visual reference.
Begin with an extreme close-up revealing the product's surface, texture, and material details. Slowly pull back to reveal the complete product, followed by a smooth cinematic orbit of approximately 30 degrees.
Use professional studio lighting, realistic reflections, soft rim lighting, elegant shadows, and sophisticated luxury advertising aesthetics.
Place the product against a clean, minimalist background with a refined colour palette that complements its original design.
Preserve the exact product shape, proportions, packaging, colours, labels, logos, and branding. Do not invent product details or modify existing text.
Camera movement must be smooth and physically believable. Maintain sharp product details, natural depth of field, and consistent lighting throughout.
No additional products, distracting props, floating objects, captions, or generated advertising text.
Output: 8-second premium product reveal, photorealistic commercial quality, 24fps, 1080p when available."
Prompt ni sesuai untuk opening iklan.
Contohnya anda jual perfume, skincare, jam tangan atau botol minuman.
Daripada gambar produk biasa, AI akan cuba hasilkan shot dengan camera movement dan lighting yang lebih premium.
Pilih ratio:
📱 9:16 untuk Reels/TikTok
🖥️ 16:9 untuk YouTube
Klik Generate.

🎬 STEP 3: PROMPT #2
PRODUCT DEMONSTRATION
Video iklan tak cukup kalau produk cuma berpusing cantik.
Kita kena tunjuk bagaimana produk itu digunakan.
Upload gambar produk yang sama supaya reka bentuk lebih konsisten.
Gantikan:
[PRODUCT] = Nama produk
[USE CASE] = Cara produk digunakan
Contoh:
[PRODUCT] = Stainless steel water bottle
[USE CASE] = A person filling the bottle with drinking water
Ini promptnya:
"Create an 8-second photorealistic product demonstration commercial featuring [PRODUCT], using the uploaded reference image to maintain visual consistency.
Show the product being used naturally in this realistic scenario: [USE CASE].
Begin with a visually engaging medium shot that establishes the product in its intended environment.
Transition into detailed close-up shots highlighting its physical design, materials, and practical usage.
Use smooth professional camera movements, including a subtle dolly-in, controlled tracking movement, and natural focus transitions.
Lighting should be soft, premium, and commercially polished, with believable reflections and accurate shadows.
Ensure realistic human interaction when applicable, natural hand movements, correct product proportions, and physically accurate object behaviour.
Preserve the original product design, colours, packaging, labels, and branding.
Only demonstrate functions supported by the actual product. Do not invent features, performance claims, or unrealistic results.
Avoid distorted hands, changing logos, inconsistent packaging, floating objects, and unnecessary text.
Output: 8-second realistic product demonstration with commercial cinematography."
Prompt ni sesuai untuk tunjuk produk digunakan dalam situasi sebenar.
Contohnya:
✅ Skincare sedang digunakan
✅ Air dituang ke dalam botol
✅ Beg dibuka dan ditutup
✅ Alat dapur digunakan
Tip: Pilih satu tindakan utama sahaja untuk setiap video 8 saat.
Lebih mudah nak dapat visual yang kemas.

🎬 STEP 4: PROMPT #3
PRODUCT CLOSING + CTA
Sekarang kita nak hasilkan scene terakhir.
Scene ni penting sebab kita nak penonton tahu produk apa yang diiklankan dan apa tindakan seterusnya.
Gunakan gambar produk yang sama.
Gantikan [PRODUCT] dengan nama produk anda.
"Create an elegant 8-second cinematic closing shot for a premium advertisement featuring [PRODUCT].
Use the uploaded reference image as the definitive guide for the product's appearance.
Position the product prominently within a beautifully composed minimalist studio environment.
Begin with a medium-wide hero shot, then execute a slow, smooth cinematic push-in toward the product.
Use soft directional studio lighting, subtle rim highlights, realistic material reflections, and elegant natural shadows.
Create a polished, sophisticated visual atmosphere with a background colour that complements the product packaging.
Maintain precise product proportions, authentic colours, accurate branding, and consistent packaging details throughout the entire scene.
Reserve generous clean negative space in the upper portion of the frame for a headline and clear space near the bottom for a call-to-action overlay.
Do not generate any text, buttons, slogans, additional logos, or unsupported product claims.
Avoid excessive visual effects, artificial motion, product distortion, distracting props, and sudden camera movements.
Finish with a stable product hero frame suitable for adding professional advertising typography during editing.
Output: 8-second cinematic closing shot, premium commercial quality, 1080p when available."
Kenapa kita tak suruh AI terus tulis headline dan CTA?
Sebab lebih mudah kawal ejaan, font, saiz dan kedudukan teks apabila kita tambah sendiri dalam editor Google Vids.
Hasilnya juga lebih mudah nak ubah untuk iklan yang berlainan.

🎞️ STEP 5: GABUNGKAN 3 VIDEO
Sekarang anda dah ada 3 scene:
1️⃣ Product Reveal
2️⃣ Product Demonstration
3️⃣ Product Closing
Masukkan ketiga-tiganya ke dalam timeline Google Vids.
Susun ikut urutan tadi.
Kalau setiap scene 8 saat, jumlah video jadi 24 saat.
Kemudian tambah:
✅ Transition yang smooth
✅ Background music yang sesuai
✅ Nama brand
✅ Benefit utama produk
✅ Headline yang pendek
✅ Call-to-action (CTA)
Contoh susunan teks:
SCENE 1
"Meet Your Everyday Essential"
SCENE 2
"Designed For Your Daily Routine"
SCENE 3
"Discover More. Shop Now."
Teks ni cuma contoh. Sesuaikan dengan produk dan manfaat sebenar.
Anda juga boleh laraskan tempoh scene dan gunakan scene extension jika ciri tersebut tersedia.
Tip saya:
Jangan letak terlalu banyak teks.
Satu mesej utama untuk setiap scene dah cukup supaya penonton mudah faham.
