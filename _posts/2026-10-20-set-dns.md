## To set your router's DNS from Google to Cloudflare

Log in to your router's administration page and replace the Google DNS addresses with Cloudflare's IPv4 or IPv6 addresses.

### Cloudflare DNS Addresses
* IPv4 (Standard): Primary: 1.1.1.1 | Secondary: 1.0.0.1
* IPv6 (Standard): Primary: 2606:4700:4700::1111 | Secondary: 2606:4700:4700::1001
* IPv4 (Malware Blocking): Primary: 1.1.1.2 | Secondary: 1.0.0.2

### Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Find your router’s login page:
    * Open a web browser on a computer or phone connected to your network.
	* Type your router's admin IP address into the address bar (common defaults include 192.168.1.1, 192.168.0.1, or router.asus.com for Asus routers).
2. Log in:
	* Enter your router's administrator username and password (check the sticker on your router or your manual if you have never changed it).
3. Locate the DNS settings:
	* Navigate to sections labeled Internet, WAN, Network Settings, DHCP, or Basic /Advanced Setup.
	* Look for the fields currently set to Google's addresses (8.8.8.8 and 8.8.4.4).
4. Enter Cloudflare DNS:
	* Switch the DNS setting from "Automatic (ISP)" or "Custom/Manual" to manual input.
	* Replace the primary DNS with 1.1.1.1.
	* Replace the secondary DNS with 1.0.0.1.
5. Save and reboot:
	* Click Save, Apply, or OK.
	* Restart your router if prompted, or disconnect and reconnect your device to refresh the network configuration.
