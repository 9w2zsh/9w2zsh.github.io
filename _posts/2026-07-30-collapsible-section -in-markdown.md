## The collapsible section

I was looking for method to have a nice and tidy page when I have several section that I need a single page. Just line Lotus Notes can do. Search the net and I found this method:
```
<details markdown="block">
  <summary>Click here to expand</summary>

Your **Markdown** content goes here.
* You can use bullet lists.
* Add `code snippets`.

</details>
```
It works well with the Jekyll blog that I have hosted on Github.
