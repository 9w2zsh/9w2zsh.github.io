## Send rig for repair
After successfully repair my ICOM IC-2027H, I've decided to send the Kenwood TM-V71A Dual Band Transceiver.  
* The issue: the IF filter was crystalized/damaged.
* The reason: in long run, any high power transmission near the radio will cause that issue. This wear & tear for this kind of radio. If possible, don't put the transceiver next to each other.
